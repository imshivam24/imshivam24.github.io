Selected Research Projects

Al-Cu single-atom alloys for CO/CO₂ reduction

Kinetics of acidic CO₂RR to C1 products on Al-Cu single-atom alloys, from DFT to microkinetic selectivity trends.

Unsteady-state microkinetic modeling

An automated framework for transient microkinetic modeling that captures the dynamics of CO reduction and oxygen evolution during potential sweeps.

Data-driven massive reaction networks

Combining DFT, machine learning, and automated reaction enumeration to build the largest known kinetic network for CO₂ hydrogenation on copper.

Bulk vs. 2D electrocatalysts for CO₂RR

A review mapping how catalyst dimensionality — bulk vs. two-dimensional — shapes the thermodynamics and kinetics of CO₂ electroreduction.

Physics-guided deep learning for nanofluid viscosity

A Physics Guided Deep Neural Network (PGDNN) that fuses theoretical viscosity models with data-driven learning to predict nanofluid viscosity.
