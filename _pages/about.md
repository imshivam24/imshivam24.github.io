Hi! I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in [Chemical Engineering](https://chemeng.iisc.ac.in/) at the [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in/), advised by Professor [Ananth Govind Rajan](https://chemeng.iisc.ac.in/faculty/ananth-govind-rajan/). My research lies at the intersection of catalysis ⚗️, computational chemistry 💻, and machine learning 🤖, with a focus on designing next-generation catalysts for sustainable chemical processes 🌱. Using density functional theory (DFT), machine learning (ML), and microkinetic modeling (MKM), I study the atomic-level mechanisms governing catalytic reactions, particularly electrochemical CO₂ reduction.

Beyond first-principles methods, I am passionate about integrating physics-based models with machine learning approaches to accelerate the discovery of efficient materials 🚀.

Alongside my research, I have served as a teaching assistant for courses on quantum-mechanical modeling (CH 251), numerical methods (CH 202), and machine learning for core engineering disciplines. I am also actively engaged in academic and student life, serving as Vice President of the Chemical Engineering Association and as Innovation Coordinator at the Institute Innovation Council.

I enjoy working on problems that combine deep theoretical insight with real-world impact 🌍 and remain curious about how emerging technologies can reshape the future of chemical engineering 🔬.
