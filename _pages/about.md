Hi! I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in [Chemical Engineering](https://chemeng.iisc.ac.in/) at the [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in/), advised by Professor [Ananth Govind Rajan](https://chemeng.iisc.ac.in/faculty/ananth-govind-rajan/). My research sits at the intersection of catalysis, computational chemistry, and machine learning: I combine density functional theory (DFT), microkinetic modeling (MKM), and ML to uncover the atomic-level mechanisms of catalytic reactions — particularly electrochemical CO₂ reduction — and to accelerate the design of next-generation catalysts for sustainable chemical processes.

Alongside my research, I have served as a teaching assistant for courses on quantum-mechanical modeling (CH 253), numerical methods (CH 202), and machine learning for core engineering disciplines. I am also actively engaged in academic and student life, serving as Vice President of the Chemical Engineering Association and as Innovation Coordinator at the Institute Innovation Council.

I enjoy problems that combine deep theoretical insight with real-world impact, and stay curious about how emerging technologies can reshape chemical engineering.
