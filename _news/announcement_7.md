Excited to share that our research article, <a href="https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cssc.202501817" target="_blank">“Hydrocarbon Selectivity in CO and CO₂ Reduction on Al–Cu Single-Atom Alloy Electrocatalysts: Effects of Potential, pH, and Facet”</a>, is now published online in ChemSusChem.

This work investigates how applied potential, electrolyte pH, and catalyst facet influence hydrocarbon selectivity during CO and CO₂ electroreduction on Al–Cu single-atom alloy surfaces, providing new mechanistic insights for electrocatalyst design.
